## Features

<details>
<summary>Miscellaneous</summary>

- **Show Updates**: Opens a panel describing plugin updates after new features are added.
- **HP Orbs**: Hides the HP orbs or replaces them with health bars.
- **Hide Fade Transition**: Hides the fade-to-black between rooms.
- **Quick Proceed**: Left-click Proceed on NPCs and quick-enter/quick-use entryways.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Lobby</summary>

- **Lobby Board Highlight**: Highlights friends' and ignores' names on the lobby party finder board.
- **Loot Reminder**: Reminds you to claim your loot when in the raid lobby.
- **Spellbook Warning**: Shows your current spellbook on the raid entrance.
- **Mute Ambience**: Mutes the looping ambient sounds in the lobby.
- **Hide Empty Party Slots**: Removes the empty '-' slots from the lobby party list, shrinking the panel to fit.
- **Mute NPC Overhead Text**: Hides the overhead text of the NPCs in the lobby.
- **Reorder Alliance Log Tabs**: Reorders the alliance records in the lobby and loot room to match the in-raid room order.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Ezekiel</summary>

- **Autocast Warning**: Highlights the entryway in red if you are on the Standard spellbook and your autocast is not a Wind spell.
- **Sword HP Bar Recolour**: Recolours the health bars of the Animated Swords.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Moia</summary>

- **Remove Walk Here**: Removes 'Walk here' and 'Examine' options outside the walkable area while inside the arena.
- **Enrage Bar Recolour**: Recolours the enrage timer bar above Moia.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Dreadnoth</summary>

- **Autocast Warning**: Highlights the entryway in red if you are on the Ancient spellbook and your autocast is not a Burst or Barrage spell.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Resupply</summary>

- **Buy Menu-Swap**: Left-click to buy from the Quartermaster.
- **Quartermaster Reminder**: Warns you to visit the Quartermaster before entering the Dagon'hai or Xarth rooms, optionally with a warning overlay and by deprioritizing the room entry options until you have.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Xarth</summary>

- **Autocast Warning**: Highlights the entryway in red if your autocast is not right for your spellbook (Water on Standard, Ice on Ancient, Grasp on Arceuus).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Ocellus</summary>

- **Autocast Warning**: Highlights the entryway in red if you are on the Standard spellbook and your autocast is not an Earth spell.
- **Start Quadrant Highlight**: Highlights the starting quadrant opposite your chosen censer.
- **Proceed Indicator**: Highlights players in red until they have clicked Proceed. Requires RL Party; non-party players can optionally be highlighted in yellow.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Loot</summary>

- **Bank-all Single Click**: Left-click only for Bank-all on the loot interface.
- **Loot Chest Highlight**: Highlights your loot chest to help quickly identify it.
- **Recolour Flames**: Recolours the loot room brazier flames when there is no purple, when the purple is yours, and when it belongs to a teammate.
- **Team and Personal Dry Streak**: Shows raids since your team's or your own last purple, in chat when you enter the loot room.
- **!tfadry Chat Command**: Responds to !tfadry in chat with the sender's dry streaks. Streaks are stored in RuneLite's !duels chat command data, so using this overwrites your !duels stats.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Points Tracker</summary>

- **Overlay**: Shows team and personal points, and team and personal unique chance. Unlike the ToA points tracker, it only updates at the end of rooms and does not require the party plugin. For accurate counts, all players must be in a RL Party with the TFA plugin.
- **Points Total Message**: Shows the total points in chat after the raid, akin to the Chambers of Xeric.
- **Send to External Plugins**: Sends the points totals to other locally installed plugins on raid completion.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Time Tracker</summary>

- **Overlay**: Shows room times on screen: the current room, past rooms, or every room.
- **Write to File**: Writes room times to a splits.txt file for each raid, in the plugin's data folder.
- **Room Infoboxes**: Shows an infobox with the time of each room after it is completed.
- **Chat Message**: Prints the time of each room to chat when Ocellus is defeated.

</details>
