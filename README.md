## Features

**Personal Purple Estimate:** A small, movable overlay projects your personal purple chance on raid completion. Enabled by default; toggle it in Loot settings. It uses observed damage and deaths, with equal shares assumed for rooms not yet observed. It is an estimate, not the server's roll: minion soft caps, Ocellus phase weights, missed hits, and changing team sizes affect accuracy. Joining mid-raid shows incomplete tracking instead of a percentage.

Rates follow the [3 November loot mechanics post](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/quest-month-kick-off-tfa-tweaks--more?oldschool=1) and may change with the announced balancing update.
